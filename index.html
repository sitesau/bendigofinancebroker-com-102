Questions before replacing a facility

The cleanest brief is short and specific. State the asset or cash-flow need, the amount, the timing, deposit position, trading history and repayment preference. Then ask what makes the file standard or non-standard, and whether specialised, used or contract-linked assets need extra evidence.

  • What exact figure is being refinanced, and does that include any remaining balloon?
  • Does the current structure leave the business owning the asset, or does the financier retain ownership?
  • Is the proposed term consistent with how long the asset will earn revenue?
  • For agriculture or seasonal trade, can repayment timing align with harvest cycles, contracts or debtor movements?

For commercial vehicle finance, flexible terms up to 7 years are stated as available, but a longer term is not automatically better in substance. Equipment finance is described as usually secured by the equipment itself, with extra security rarely required for standard applications; that does not remove the need for a tidy file.

Structures that change the refinance answer

The named structures matter because the tax and ownership logic can move the after-cost result. Under a chattel mortgage, the source says the borrower owns the asset from day one, may claim GST upfront and may benefit from depreciation deductions. Under a finance lease, the lender owns the asset, GST is charged on repayments rather than upfront, and the end choice can be return or purchase. Hire purchase belongs in the same comparison set, even though the page gives less detail, because usage, ownership preference and balance-sheet treatment should be checked before signing.

The prudent path is to model each option with an accountant. GST, depreciation, cash flow and the way the asset is used can matter as much as a small rate gap. A balloon may reduce regular repayments, yet the final payment still needs a plan through sale, retained profit, trade-in or another facility.

What to compare across a 30 plus panel

A broad panel of major banks, non-banks and specialist equipment financiers is useful only when the brief is comparable. Keep the asset, amount, term, balloon, deposit and security constant, then ask for the total repayment picture and the conditions attached. A cheaper monthly figure can become less attractive once the term stretches beyond asset life or the end value is unclear.

The stated bands frame the conversation rather than promise outcomes: $10,000 to $5 million for unsecured or secured business loans, and $20,000 to $500,000 plus for working capital. Smaller unsecured loans may move faster, while complexity, documentation and asset type can extend assessment.

Common questions

Does refinancing remove the need for documents? No. The stated process still depends on identification, business details, asset quotes or invoices, basic financial information and notes that explain seasonal trading or uneven cash flow. Clean paperwork supports the stated pre-approval and full approval windows.

Is 24 hour approval guaranteed? The source states pre-approval is often available within 24 hours for standard applications. Full approval is typically 2 to 5 business days, and smaller unsecured loans may be faster. Complexity, documents and asset type can change the timeline.

Should the same structure be kept? Not automatically. A chattel mortgage, finance lease and hire purchase can place ownership, GST and deductions differently. Test the replacement with an accountant before signing, especially where a balloon or asset disposal is part of the plan.
